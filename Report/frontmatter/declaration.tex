\chapter*{Declaration}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Declaration}

We hereby declare that the work presented in this report, titled
\textbf{\ProjectTitle}, is the outcome of our own investigation carried out
under the supervision of \Supervisors. Wherever contributions of others are
involved, every effort has been made to acknowledge this clearly with due
reference to the literature. This work has not been submitted elsewhere for
the award of any degree or diploma.

The Persuasion-For-Good donation-solicitation dialogues used in this project
originate from the publicly released corpus of \citet{wang2019persuasion};
our own contribution is the taxonomy, the multi-label persuasion-strategy
annotation of 10{,}600 persuader turns, and all classification, ensembling,
and statistical analysis built on top of it, as described in
Chapter~\ref{ch:design}. Portions of the strategy-level and dialogue-level
annotation were produced with the assistance of a large language model under
a documented human-verification checkpoint (Section~\ref{sec:annotation});
this is disclosed here and again where the annotation methodology is
described in full.
