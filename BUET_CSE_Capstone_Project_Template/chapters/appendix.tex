\section*{XGBoost Decision Stacker Tuning Grid}
The tuned XGBoost stacking decision layer evaluated across 72 candidate grid configurations:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Feature Regimes:} Logits Mode (20 features) and Rich Mode (41 features).
  \item \textbf{Max Depth:} $\in \{3, 4, 6\}$ (winning value: 4).
  \item \textbf{Number of Estimators:} $\in \{100, 300, 500\}$ (winning: 300 for macro, 500 for micro).
  \item \textbf{Learning Rate ($\eta$):} $\in \{0.03, 0.06, 0.10\}$ (winning value: 0.06).
  \item \textbf{Subsample / Colsample:} $0.80$ / $0.80$.
  \item \textbf{Positive Weight Multiplier:} Scaled inverse frequency with a hard cap of $10.0$.
\end{itemize}

\chapter{Conflict Register and Empirical Audit Log}
\label{app:conflicts}

To ensure complete scientific integrity, this appendix documents the resolution of all fifteen empirical and textual discrepancies identified in the project record:

\begin{keypoint}[Audit Rule of Primacy: Ground-Truth Empirical Primacy]
Whenever prompt narratives, retrospective slides, or draft summaries contradict raw serialized artifacts (\texttt{.jsonl}, \texttt{.csv}, evaluation outputs, or reproducible notebook execution cells), the empirical data artifacts are held strictly authoritative. Speculative claims and draft misstatements are formally audited and corrected.
\end{keypoint}
